<h1>Troubleshooting-Outlook</h1>


<h2>Description</h2>
The purpose of this lab is to demonstrate how to troubleshoot Outlook startup issues caused by add-ins.


<h2>Utilities Used</h2>

- <b>Microsoft 365</b>


<h2>Environments Used </h2>

- <b>Windows 10 </b>
- <b>Outlook</b>


<h2>Program walk-through:</h2>

The first step I took was to open Outlook in Safe Mode. This is an effective troubleshooting method, as it helps isolate any add-ins or applications that may be causing startup issues.

<img src="https://i.imgur.com/YF64AzV.png" height="80%" width="80%" alt="Disk Sanitization Steps"/>

Once in Safe Mode, navigate to File, then Options, and select the Add-ins section.

<img src="https://i.imgur.com/bzYAPB2.png" height="80%" width="80%" alt="Disk Sanitization Steps"/>

From here, you can disable various add-ins to determine if any of them are preventing Outlook from opening.
